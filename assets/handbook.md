# ISUFST Student Handbook

## About ISUFST

The Iloilo State University of Fisheries Science and Technology (ISUFST) is a state university committed to providing quality, relevant, and accessible higher education to the people of Iloilo and the Western Visayas Region. ISUFST operates under the governance of the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and is guided by its vision of becoming a premier university in fisheries and allied sciences, producing globally competitive graduates who are imbued with Filipino values and a deep commitment to sustainable development of the country's aquatic and marine resources.

The university offers programs across various colleges including the College of Fisheries, College of Engineering and Technology, College of Business and Management, College of Arts and Sciences, College of Education, and College of Information and Computing Sciences, among others.

## Enrollment and Registration

### Enrollment Periods

ISUFST follows the academic calendar prescribed by CHED. Enrollment for the first semester typically takes place in June, while second semester enrollment is conducted in October or November. Summer term enrollment is held in March or April.

Students must enroll within the designated enrollment period. Late enrollment is allowed within the grace period set by the Registrar's Office, subject to a late enrollment fee of ₱100.00 per day.

### Pre-Enrollment Requirements

New students must present the following documents at the Registrar's Office:
- Form 138 (Report Card) or Transcript of Records for transferees
- Certificate of Good Moral Character from the last school attended
- PSA-issued Birth Certificate
- Two (2) pieces 2×2 ID photos
- Medical certificate from a licensed physician
- Barangay clearance
- Original copy of NCAE results (for incoming freshmen)

Transferees are additionally required to submit a Certificate of Transfer and Honorable Dismissal, and must pass any qualifying examination prescribed by their intended college.

### Cross-Enrollment

Students who wish to cross-enroll in another SUC or CHED-recognized institution must secure a cross-enrollment permit from the Registrar's Office of ISUFST before enrolling in the host school. Units earned through cross-enrollment are credited upon submission of the official transcript and certificate of grades from the host institution.

## Attendance Policy

Regular attendance is a fundamental expectation for all ISUFST students. Students are required to attend no less than 80% of the total prescribed class hours per subject. Failure to meet this requirement may result in automatic dropping of the subject.

### Excused and Unexcused Absences

An absence is considered excused if supported by any of the following documentation submitted to the subject instructor within three (3) school days of returning:
- Medical certificate from a licensed physician or rural health unit
- Written affidavit from a parent or guardian for family emergencies
- Official university documentation for authorized school activities (e.g., off-campus events, sports competitions, cultural presentations)

Unexcused absences are those not covered by any supporting documentation. Three (3) consecutive unexcused absences may prompt the instructor to file a dropping recommendation with the Dean's Office.

### Tardiness

A student who arrives more than 15 minutes after the scheduled start of class is considered late. Three (3) tardiness marks are equivalent to one (1) absence. Students who are late must still be allowed inside the classroom but should be recorded as tardy in the class record.

### Leave of Absence

Students who need to be absent for an extended period (e.g., due to illness, personal hardship, or participation in a national-level competition) may apply for an official Leave of Absence (LOA) through the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS). An LOA must be filed before the period of absence whenever possible. Approved LOAs will not count against the student's attendance record.

## Grading System

ISUFST follows the grading system prescribed by its academic policies in accordance with CHED guidelines.

### Numerical Grading Scale

| Grade | Percentage Equivalent | Description |
|-------|----------------------|-------------|
| 1.00  | 97–100%              | Excellent    |
| 1.25  | 94–96%               | Superior     |
| 1.50  | 91–93%               | Very Good    |
| 1.75  | 88–90%               | Good         |
| 2.00  | 85–87%               | Very Satisfactory |
| 2.25  | 82–84%               | Satisfactory |
| 2.50  | 79–81%               | Fair         |
| 2.75  | 76–78%               | Passing      |
| 3.00  | 75%                  | Minimum Passing |
| 5.00  | Below 75%            | Failing      |
| INC   | —                    | Incomplete   |
| DRP   | —                    | Dropped      |

A grade of 3.00 is the minimum passing mark. A grade of 5.00 (Failing) requires the student to retake the subject. The grade of INC (Incomplete) is given when a student has not completed all required coursework due to justifiable reasons; the student must complete the requirements within one academic year, otherwise the INC is converted to 5.00.

### Grade Computation

Final grades are computed based on the following components, unless otherwise specified in the subject syllabus:
- Quizzes and Seatwork: 30%
- Major Examinations (Midterm and Final): 40%
- Term Output / Laboratory Work / Projects: 20%
- Class Participation and Attendance: 10%

### Grade Review

A student who wishes to question or verify their grade must submit a written Grade Consultation Request to the subject instructor within ten (10) school days of the official release of grades. If the concern is unresolved, the student may elevate it to the Department Chair, then to the College Dean, following the prescribed grievance procedure.

## Late Submission Policy

Assignments, projects, and other required outputs must be submitted on or before the deadline set by the instructor. Late submissions are penalized as follows:

- 1–2 school days late: 10% deduction from the earned score
- 3–5 school days late: 20% deduction from the earned score
- More than 5 school days late: a maximum score of 50% of the total points may be given, at the instructor's discretion

Extensions may be requested in writing at least 24 hours before the deadline. The instructor may grant an extension without penalty in cases of documented medical emergencies, natural calamities (such as typhoons or flooding), or approved participation in university-sponsored activities. Students enrolled under the Persons with Disability (PWD) accommodation plan or under a Special Academic Arrangement may have adjusted submission guidelines as specified in their plans.

## Dress Code and Uniform Policy

ISUFST students are required to wear the prescribed university uniform during all academic activities. Wearing the proper uniform is a matter of discipline and institutional pride.

### Prescribed Uniform

- **Male Students:** White polo shirt (with the official ISUFST logo), dark slacks, leather shoes or clean rubber shoes, and a plain white undershirt.
- **Female Students:** White blouse (with the official ISUFST logo), dark slacks or modest dark skirt (knee-length or below), leather shoes or clean rubber shoes, and a plain white undershirt.
- **Physical Education (PE) Classes:** Authorized PE uniform consisting of the university-issued T-shirt and jogging pants.

The official university ID must be worn at all times within the campus premises.

### Prohibited Attire

The following are not allowed on campus during academic hours:
- Sleeveless shirts, spaghetti straps, or revealing tops
- Shorts (except during PE class)
- Ripped, torn, or distressed clothing
- Slippers or sandals (except for documented health or medical reasons)
- Caps or hats inside classrooms or the library
- Clothing bearing offensive, vulgar, or politically divisive text or imagery

Students found violating the dress code will be advised by the Security Guard or any university personnel and may be denied entry to classrooms until properly dressed. Persistent violations are reported to the OSAS for appropriate disciplinary action.

## Examination Rules and Procedures

### Schedule and Announcement

The midterm examination schedule is released by the Office of the Registrar at least two (2) weeks before the exam week. The final examination schedule is released at least three (3) weeks in advance. Schedules are posted on official bulletin boards and the university's official social media pages and website.

### Examination Permits

Students must secure an Examination Permit from the Cashier's Office prior to any major examination. The examination permit serves as proof that the student has no outstanding financial obligations. Instructors are required to check examination permits before admitting students to the examination room.

Students who have not settled their fees will not be issued an examination permit. They may appeal to the Finance Office for a temporary permit under exceptional circumstances.

### Conduct During Examinations

- Students must arrive at least five (5) minutes before the scheduled examination.
- Late students may be admitted only within the first 30 minutes of the examination, without additional time.
- Mobile phones, tablets, smartwatches, earphones, and other electronic devices must be turned off and placed inside bags. Violations will be treated as cheating.
- Students are prohibited from bringing notes, printed materials, or any unauthorized reference materials inside the examination room.
- Communication between students during the examination is strictly prohibited.
- Any student caught cheating will receive a grade of 0.00 for that examination and will be subject to disciplinary proceedings.

### Special Examinations

A student who was absent during a scheduled examination may request a Special Examination within five (5) school days of returning to school. The request must be supported by an official medical certificate, an affidavit of a parent or guardian, or other documentary proof acceptable to the instructor. Special examinations are given at the instructor's discretion and schedule.

## School Fees and Financial Matters

As a State University, ISUFST implements the Free Higher Education Act (Republic Act No. 10931) also known as the Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act. Filipino students enrolled in baccalaureate programs at ISUFST are entitled to free tuition and other school fees under this law.

### Fees That May Still Apply

Certain fees are not covered by RA 10931 and remain the responsibility of the student:

| Fee | Amount |
|-----|--------|
| Miscellaneous Fee | ₱500.00 – ₱1,500.00 per semester (varies by program) |
| Laboratory Fee | ₱300.00 – ₱800.00 per semester (for laboratory-intensive programs) |
| Medical/Dental Fee | ₱100.00 per semester |
| Library Fee | ₱150.00 per semester |
| Student Organization Fee | ₱200.00 per year |
| ID Fee (new students) | ₱150.00 (one-time) |
| Athletic Fee | ₱150.00 per year |
| Development Fee | ₱500.00 per year |

### Scholarships and Financial Assistance

Students may apply for the following scholarships and financial assistance programs:
- **UNIFAST/TES (Tertiary Education Subsidy):** Available to students from low-income households (LISTAHANAN beneficiaries). Provides a monthly stipend of up to ₱1,200.00.
- **CHED Merit Scholarship:** Awarded to students who passed the CHED Scholarship Qualifying Examination and meet the academic requirements.
- **Local Government Unit (LGU) Scholarships:** Various municipalities and the Provincial Government of Iloilo offer scholarships for their constituents enrolled at ISUFST.
- **Student Assistant Program (SAP):** Students may apply for a working scholar arrangement, rendering services to the university in exchange for a living allowance.
- **Private and Corporate Scholarships:** The OSAS maintains a list of available private scholarships that students may apply for.

Applications for scholarships and financial assistance must be submitted to the OSAS or the Scholarship Office at the beginning of each semester. Late applications will not be entertained.

### Payment of Fees

All fees must be paid at the Cashier's Office during the designated enrollment period. The university accepts payment in cash. Students must keep official receipts for all transactions. Fees, once paid, are generally non-refundable, except in the case of an officially approved LOA or withdrawal from the university before the end of the first week of regular classes.

## Library Rules and Regulations

The ISUFST Main Library and college-level libraries are open to all bonafide students, faculty, and staff. Library hours are from 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM on regular school days. During examination periods, the library may extend its hours as announced.

### Borrowing Privileges

Students may borrow a maximum of three (3) books at a time for a period of three (3) school days, with one (1) renewal allowed provided the material is not reserved by another student. Reserve materials and reference books (encyclopedias, indexes, theses, dissertations) are for room use only and cannot be taken out of the library.

### Fines and Penalties

- Overdue books: ₱5.00 per day per book
- Lost books: Replacement with a copy of the same title or edition, plus a processing fee of ₱100.00
- Damaged materials: Replacement cost as assessed by the librarian

Students with unsettled library obligations will not be issued a library clearance, which is required for enrollment, examination permits, and graduation.

## Student Organizations

ISUFST recognizes the importance of co-curricular and extra-curricular activities in the holistic development of students. All recognized student organizations must be registered with the OSAS at the beginning of each academic year.

The Supreme Student Government (SSG) is the highest student governing body in the university and represents the interests of all enrolled students. All students are automatic members of the SSG and are encouraged to participate in elections and SSG activities.

Recognized student organizations may request the use of university facilities for their activities by submitting a written request at least one (1) week in advance to the OSAS and the Office of the Campus Administrator. Organizations must comply with all university policies and applicable laws, including Republic Act No. 8049 (Anti-Hazing Law), as amended.

## Health and Safety

ISUFST maintains a Health Services Unit (HSU) staffed by a licensed physician and registered nurses. The HSU provides first aid, basic consultations, and emergency response within the campus.

Students who feel unwell during class must inform their instructor and proceed to the HSU for assessment. The HSU may issue a medical certificate for absences arising from illness diagnosed and treated on-campus.

In the event of a declared public health emergency (such as during the COVID-19 pandemic or other IATF-declared health emergencies), the university will implement the health protocols prescribed by the Department of Health (DOH), CHED, and the Local Inter-Agency Task Force (IATF). These may include health declarations, face mask requirements, physical distancing measures, or the shifting of classes to flexible or online learning modalities.

## Procedures for Dropping and Withdrawal

### Dropping a Subject

A student may officially drop a subject without academic penalty within the period prescribed by CHED (generally up to the end of the preliminary period or as specified in the academic calendar). To drop a subject, the student must:
1. Secure a Drop Form from the Registrar's Office.
2. Obtain the signatures of the subject instructor, Department Chair, and College Dean.
3. Submit the completed form to the Registrar's Office before the deadline.

After the prescribed dropping period, a subject may still be dropped due to medical or other extraordinary reasons, with the approval of the Dean. A grade of "DRP" (Dropped) will appear on the student's record.

### Withdrawal from the University

A student who wishes to withdraw from enrollment must file a formal withdrawal at the Registrar's Office. Required clearances must be obtained from various offices (library, finance, OSAS, dormitory if applicable) before the withdrawal is finalized. A Certificate of Transfer and Honorable Dismissal will be issued to the withdrawing student after the clearance process is complete.
